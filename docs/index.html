14.8%typical miss

ZIP Code-Aware Sale Price Prediction

Regression · Gradient boosting

Buyers, sellers, and agents need a price before an offer. A national median misses the point that an extra bathroom is not worth the same amount in every ZIP. Sales run from October 18, 2021, through May 6, 2022.

On ZIP codes with at least 20 training sales, gradient boosting is typically off by 14.8 percent, about 99,000 dollars. A model with no location is typically off by 36.5 percent. Below 20 training sales, the recommendation is to withhold a firm number. The fit is a screening tool beside recent comps, not an appraisal.

0.78recall

Telecom Customer Churn Prediction

Classification · Random forest

A telecommunications company needs to know which customers are likely to cancel, and which retention actions are worth taking, before those customers leave. The file is the public IBM Telco set, 7,043 customers.

The random forest is the recommended model: recall 0.78, AUC-ROC 0.84, and F1 0.63. Contract type, early tenure, fiber service, and missing add-on services are the actionable patterns. Precision is about 0.53, so the score fits a low-cost outreach pilot better than an expensive discount for everyone the model flags.

0.77holdout macro F1

Tariff Announcement Market-Reaction Classification

Classification · Random forest

Tariff announcements do not move every industry the same way. The prepared table has 362 sector-event rows, joining daily sector ETF returns to major tariff actions from 2018 through 2025. The same-day broad-market return was kept out of the features.

Random forest was the best model, with cross-validated macro F1 of 0.781 and a holdout macro F1 of 0.774 (ROC-AUC 0.835, accuracy 0.795). Recent market volatility and sector momentum mattered at least as much as the size of the tariff.

16%of household income

American Childcare Affordability

Public data · Visualization

Families and policymakers need a plain measure of whether infant center-based care is affordable. The metric is annual cost as a share of median household income, set against the 7 percent policy benchmark. The source is the U.S. Department of Labor database, county-years from 2008 through 2018.

The median county-year burden is about 16 percent, and more than 99 percent of valid county-years exceed 7 percent. No state clears the line. The link to female labor-force participation is weak, about -0.06, so cost alone does not explain whether mothers work.

115North Carolina districts

North Carolina School Finance Data Integration

SQL · Public data

District spending, student outcomes, and child poverty live in different public files. The project cleans the NCES F-33 finance survey for fiscal year 2023, Public School Review district pages, and Census SAIPE estimates, then joins them in SQLite on the NCES district id.

Per-pupil spending by itself is a weak predictor of math proficiency. Child poverty has a strong negative relationship with graduation rate, and median household income lines up with higher reading proficiency. Lower-income districts rely more on federal and state revenue.

0.96R-squared

Combined-Cycle Power Plant Output

Regression · Clustering

Plant operators need to know which ambient conditions drive net electrical output. The UCI Combined Cycle Power Plant file has nearly 10,000 full-load hourly observations from 2006 through 2011. Predictors are temperature, exhaust vacuum, ambient pressure, and relative humidity.

Temperature and exhaust vacuum dominate output, with correlations about -0.95 and -0.87. Linear regression reaches an R-squared near 0.93. Random forest improves that to about 0.96, with RMSE about 3.31 MW. The plant also separates into low, medium, and high output regimes.

10similar titles

Movie Recommendation System

Collaborative filtering

A catalog needs a way to suggest the next movie from how people have rated titles, without relying only on genre labels. MovieLens Small has 100,836 ratings of 9,742 movies by 610 users. The model keeps movies with at least 10 ratings and ranks candidates by cosine similarity.

Given a title, the system returns the 10 most similar movies. Franchise titles and films from a similar era cluster together. The matrix is about 98 percent empty, so new titles and new users are a cold-start problem, and a full similarity matrix would not be the production design.

4 hubslead the volume

TSA Complaint Trends

Visualization · Story

Travelers need a practical view of where and when airport-security complaints pile up. TSA complaint releases by airport, category, and subcategory were joined to a public airport-code table. The piece is a visual brief: map, heat map, and box plot.

LAX, JFK, ORD, and SFO lead complaint volume. Damaged or missing checked baggage is the largest subcategory, and summer months spike at major hubs. The recommendation is extra time at those airports, valuables in a carry-on, and a filed complaint when screening or baggage handling goes wrong.
